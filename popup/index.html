Life gets overwhelming sometimes, and even a tiny moment of joy can make a difference. Destress with Animals was created to give you quick, comforting breaks throughout your day. Each time you open the extension, you’ll see a randomly generated image of an adorable animal—something soft, silly, or heart-melting. These small bursts of cuteness can help ease stress, lift your mood, and remind you to pause and breathe. Think of it as a pocket-sized happiness button right in your browser.
