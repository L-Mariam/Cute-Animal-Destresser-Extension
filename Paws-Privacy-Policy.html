Privacy Policy

Destress with Cute Animals Chrome Extension
Last Updated:

Welcome to Paws! We're committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your personal data.

TL;DR: Paws stores data locally on your device, doesn't collect personal information, and only connects to Pexels API when you provide your own API key.

What Data We Collect

Paws collects minimal data, all stored locally on your device:

  • Saved Images: URLs and metadata of animal pictures you save for later viewing
  • Hidden Images: IDs of images you choose to hide (so they don't appear again)
  • Pexels API Key: Only if you choose to provide one for accessing animal images
  • Extension Settings: Your preference settings stored locally
All data is stored locally in Chrome's storage

How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to provide and improve your experience with Paws:

  • Display your saved animal pictures for quick access
  • Remember which images you've hidden to personalize your feed
  • Authenticate with Pexels API (only with your provided API key)
  • Maintain your preferences across browser sessions

Third-Party Services

Paws uses the Pexels API to fetch animal images. When you provide your Pexels API key:

  • Your key is used only to authenticate with Pexels API
  • API requests are made directly from your browser to Pexels servers
  • We don't have access to your Pexels account or API key
  • Pexels' privacy policy applies to their service

Pexels Privacy Policy: https://www.pexels.com/privacy-policy/

Never share your API key with others

No Personal Data Collection

We want to be perfectly clear about what we don't collect:

  • No personal identification information
  • No email addresses or contact details
  • No browsing history outside the extension
  • No location data
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No data sharing with advertisers

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Chrome's built-in storage APIs:

  • Local Storage: Data persists on your device until you clear browser data
  • Sync Storage (Optional): If enabled, data syncs across your Chrome browsers
  • Encryption: Chrome handles encryption of stored data
  • Deletion: Clear all data by removing the extension or clearing browser data

To delete your data from Paws:

  1. Go to Chrome Extensions (chrome://extensions/)
  2. Find Paws and click "Remove"
  3. Or use Chrome Settings to clear extension data

Policy Changes

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Significant changes will be communicated through:

  • Extension update notes in Chrome Web Store
  • Updated version notification in the extension
